Clinic Appointments

Background
Patients book appointments. Reception manages availability. This document does not specify an AI assistant.

1. Requirements
1. Patients may request an available slot.
2. Appointment Management requires an appointment record and owns booking confirmation.
3. If the slot is no longer available, offer another slot instead of confirming.
4. Patient information must not appear in public appointment views.

Acceptance criteria
Then a rejected booking must not create a confirmed appointment.
