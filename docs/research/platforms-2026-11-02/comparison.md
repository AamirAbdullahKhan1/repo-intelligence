# Comparative technical matrix

Date: 2 November 2026. Read the individual reports for source links and qualifications. Cells summarize those findings; no new tool execution or measured accuracy ranking is implied.

**Verified (V):** inspected source or formal schema establishes the mechanism. **Documented (D):** official product material describes it, not independently reproduced. **Inferred (I):** source-informed interpretation. **Unknown (U):** insufficient inspected evidence. Labels are about evidence, not feature quality. “Not established” is not proof of absence. ## What not to conclude

* No row establishes complete architecture recovery, runtime behavior or enterprise performance.
* A product's support list and a rich schema are not evidence that every construct is extracted correctly.
* Sourcegraph and StructSAR solve different stages from GitDiagram; a single overall “winner” would be misleading.
* Our compiler-bound local calls are a useful strength, not proof of superior overall recall or architecture quality.
* Reproducible inference requires pinned inputs/configurations and defined semantics, not just fixed graph IDs or seeds.
